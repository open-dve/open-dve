# I2C - protocol summary for verification

Sections read for this agent: 3.1.1-3.1.6 (signals, data validity, START/STOP,
byte format, ACK/NACK), 3.1.9 (clock stretching), 3.1.10-3.1.14 (addressing,
10-bit addressing, reserved addresses Table 4, general call, software reset),
Table 11 (Standard/Fast/Fast-mode Plus timing). Newer UM10204 revisions use
"controller/target" for the older "master/slave"; this agent keeps
`master`/`slave` for the agent roles (framework convention) and uses
controller/target in protocol text.

## Signals and electrical model

| Signal | Driven by | Type |
| --- | --- | --- |
| SCL | controller (clock), target (stretching only) | open drain, pull-up |
| SDA | transmitter of the current bit; receiver during the acknowledge | open drain, pull-up |

Both lines are wired-AND: any device can pull a line low, nobody drives it
high. The testbench models this without tristates (Verilator): every party
has a `*_o`/`*_oe` pair in `odve_i2c_if` (`m_` master agent, `s_` slave agent,
`d_` DUT, `x_` spare) and the interface resolves
`scl = ~(m_pull | s_pull | d_pull | x_pull)` (pull = `oe & ~o`). The RTL
target has separate `scl_i/scl_o/scl_oe`, `sda_i/sda_o/sda_oe` ports.

All parties work on one oversampling clock `clk` (10 ns); bus times are
counted in its cycles. The default scale is 1 cycle = 0.5 us of a
Standard-mode (100 kHz) bus, i.e. one SCL period = 20 cycles.

## Bus conditions and frame formats

```
           START (S)                      repeated START (Sr)       STOP (P)
SDA  ------\_____ ... ____/------\________ ...        _________/------
SCL  ---------\__ ... __/----------\_____ ...  __/---------------
           |tHD;STA|          |tSU;STA|tHD;STA|      |tSU;STO|  tBUF ...
```
SDA changes only while SCL is LOW (3.1.3); a HIGH-to-LOW SDA transition
while SCL is HIGH is START, LOW-to-HIGH is STOP (3.1.4).

One byte (3.1.5, 3.1.6): 8 data bits MSB first, then the acknowledge clock,
during which the transmitter releases SDA and the receiver pulls it low (ACK)
or leaves it high (NACK). A target may hold SCL low after the acknowledge
until it is ready (clock stretching, 3.1.9):

```
SCL  __/-\__/-\__ ... __/-\__/-\_______________/-\__
SDA  <b7 ><b6 >   ...  <b0 ><ACK>               <b7 ...
                                 \__ target holds SCL low (stretch)
```

Transfer formats (3.1.10, 3.1.11):

```
write        S | A6..A0 W | A | D | A | ... | D | A/N | P
read         S | A6..A0 R | A | D | A | ... | D | N | P          (controller NACKs the last byte)
combined     S | A W | A | D | A ... Sr | A R | A | D | A ... | D | N | P
10-bit write S | 11110 a9 a8 W | A1 | a7..a0 | A2 | D | A ... P
10-bit read  S | 11110 a9 a8 W | A1 | a7..a0 | A2 | Sr | 11110 a9 a8 R | A3 | D | A ... | D | N | P
general call S | 0000000 W | A | 06h | A | P                     (software reset, 3.1.14)
```

## Addressing

- 7-bit addresses; `0000 XXX` and `1111 XXX` are reserved (Table 4):
  `0000 000 W` general call, `0000 000 R` START byte (nobody acknowledges),
  `1111 0XX` 10-bit addressing header.
- 10-bit: the header carries a9..a8; every 10-bit target whose high bits match
  acknowledges A1, only the one whose low byte matches acknowledges A2; after
  Sr the header with R reaches the target that was addressed (A3).
- General call: the second byte says what to do: 06h reset + take in the
  programmable address part, 04h take in the address part without reset; other
  even codes are ignored (not acknowledged).

## Error / abort conditions

- NACK (3.1.6): no target at the address; target busy; data it cannot accept;
  end of a read (controller-receiver).
- START or STOP inside a byte: an illegal format (a byte has 8 bits + ACK, SDA
  may change only while SCL is LOW); a target must drop the partial byte.
- Arbitration loss (3.1.8): a transmitter that sends a 1 and reads a 0.

## Timing parameters modelled (clk cycles, defaults of `odve_i2c_cfg`)

| Parameter | UM10204 Table 11 (Sm) | Generated by the master | Checked by the monitor (min) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| tLOW | 4.7 us | 10 | 10 |
| tHIGH | 4.0 us | 10 | 8 |
| tHD;STA | 4.0 us | 10 | 8 |
| tSU;STA | 4.7 us | 10 | - |
| tSU;STO | 4.0 us | 10 | 8 |
| tBUF | 4.7 us | 10 | 10 |
| tHD;DAT / tSU;DAT | 0 / 250 ns | SDA set tLOW/2 after SCL falls | - (data validity checked instead) |

The RTL target synchronizes SCL/SDA with two flops and answers about three
clk cycles after an SCL edge, well inside tLOW/2.

## Modelled / not modelled

Modelled: START, repeated START, STOP; 7-bit and 10-bit addressing (write,
read, combined); ACK/NACK of every byte; controller NACK of the last read
byte; clock stretching by the target after the acknowledge; general call
(06h software reset; other codes NACKed); START byte not acknowledged;
STOP inside a byte (abort) and recovery; SDA ownership per bit; the timing
minima above; an SMBus-style clock-low timeout in the target (off for I2C,
used by smbus/pmbus).

Not modelled: multi-controller arbitration and clock synchronization (3.1.7,
3.1.8 - one controller only; the master only flags a 1 read back as 0), the
START byte procedure (3.1.15), bus clear (3.1.16), Device ID (3.1.17),
Hs-mode, Fast-mode Plus and Ultra Fast-mode (Section 5), general call 04h
(programmable address) and the hardware general call, electrical levels,
rise/fall times, spike suppression (tSP).
